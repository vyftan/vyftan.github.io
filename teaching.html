Teaching Philosophy

I enjoy teaching mathematically rigorous courses that connect theory with modern applications. My goal is to help students develop intuition alongside mathematical precision, while cultivating independent thinking and problem-solving skills. Over the years, I have taught undergraduate and graduate courses in information theory, machine learning, optimization, stochastic processes, mathematical analysis, and data modelling, ranging from small graduate classes to lectures with more than 700 students.
